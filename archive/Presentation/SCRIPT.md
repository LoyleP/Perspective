# Perspective - Presentation Script (2m30s)

**Note**: Read naturally. This is written script—adapt to your voice. Timing in brackets.

---

## SLIDE 1: Hook + Problem [0:00-0:20]

> "Same story. Opposite headlines. Sound familiar?
>
> French news is fragmented across political lines. Readers stick to their bubble. They never see how the *other side* covers the story.
>
> Result: polarization, filter bubbles, zero perspective."

**[Pause 1 second]**

---

## SLIDE 2: The Concept [0:20-0:45]

> "Perspective fixes this.
>
> One story, *every* angle.
>
> We aggregate French news articles covering the same event, map each source's political lean—extrême-gauche to extrême-droite—and show you the full spectrum in one place.
>
> **[Pause]** Not just aggregation. Aggregation with *political context*.
>
> So you can see what Le Figaro says *and* what Mediapart says, side-by-side."

**[Launch app in simulator now]**

---

## SLIDE 3: Origin Story [0:45-1:05]

> "I noticed the problem: in France, readers stay in their bubble. They never see how different parts of the political spectrum cover the same story.
>
> Nobody's built a solution for this.
>
> French media landscape is unique—strong political identities, distinct ownership models.
>
> So I built Perspective: one app to break filter bubbles in French news."

**[Keep feed visible in simulator]**

---

## SLIDE 4: Feed Demo [1:05-1:30]

> "Open the app. You see today's top stories.
>
> Each story shows:
> - How many sources covered it
> - The political breakdown—this bar chart showing left, center, right distribution
> - One tap, you're in.
>
> Hero story at the top. Daily brief below. Zero clutter. Just signal."

**[Scroll feed slowly in simulator - show hero, brief, main feed]**

## SLIDE 5: Story Detail Demo [1:30-2:00]

> "Inside a story: articles grouped by political lean.
>
> Read the left's take, read the right's take, read center coverage. All in one scroll.
>
> AI-generated spectrum summary, powered by Gemini: 'Here's how the left frames it. Here's the right's angle.'
>
> No more guessing. No more accidental bubbles.
>
> And we show ownership: this source is owned by a billionaire. This one's independent. Context matters."

**[Tap story → scroll to spectrum summary → scroll to articles → scroll to ownership]**
